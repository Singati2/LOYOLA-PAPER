## Exactness conventions

- **Trees.** All non-isomorphic trees of orders 2–17 come from `networkx.nonisomorphic_trees`. The counts are 1, 1, 2, 3, 6, 11, 23, 47, 106, 235, 551, 1301, 3159, 7741, 19320, 48629, which match OEIS A000055. "mol" means the subset with Δ ≤ 4.
- **Sums with rational exponents.** Sums of the form Σ m (ij)^a (i+j)^b are reduced exactly to the Q-basis {N^{1/k}: N k-th-power-free}, which is linearly independent over Q by Besicovitch's theorem. Distinctness is therefore decided exactly.
- **Algebraic γ.** At γ = 1 or 2, equality of two values is decided by comparing the coefficient of each imbalance class (Lindemann–Weierstrass, as in Prop. offplane).
- **Numerical cross-check.** Every exact count was recomputed with mpmath at 50 digits, treating values as distinct when they differ by more than 1e-40. For the generic point (0.3, 0.2, 0), whose values are not algebraic, the count is numerical only. The smallest gap between distinct values was ≥ 5.3e-7, far above the working precision.
- **Profiles vs trees.** "Profiles" means distinct degree-pair multisets and "trees" means isomorphism classes. Both are reported where the paper distinguishes them.

## Notes (not refutations)

1. **Inconsistent wording of the enumeration range.** main 632 says minimality "rests on an exhaustive enumeration of all trees of orders 2 to 16". main 709 and the Table 2 caption (1082) say 2–17. Both are true, since 2–16 is enough for the minimality claims and 2–17 was also enumerated, but a reader may notice the mismatch.
2. **Notation in Table 2.** In the "merges" column for GA, "17" (all trees) and "> 17" (Δ ≤ 4) are both exact. GA's "below floor" entry (566/570 at 13) coincides with the pure-γ entry because GA merges no histograms below 17. Both points are as stated in footnote c.
3. **Comparison with committed outputs.** `structural/minimality_check_out.txt`, `exact_ties_out.txt` and `chi_floor_out.txt` agree with every number recomputed here. The comparison was made after the independent computation.
