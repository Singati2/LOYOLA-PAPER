# Short version against full version: changelog (2026-10-17)

* **Full version:** `main.tex` (43 A5 pages) and `supplement.tex` (28 pages).
* **Short version:** `drafts/short/main_short.tex` (24 pages) and `drafts/short/supplement_short.tex`
  (33 pages). Both are generated by scripts in this folder from `body.tex`, `main.tex` and
  `supplement.tex`. The full files are never modified by the scripts.

## Build

```bash
cd drafts/short
python3 build.py              # main_short.tex: preamble of ../../main.tex (only \title differs), body.tex, cited bibitems
python3 build_supplement.py   # supplement_short.tex from ../../supplement.tex (renumbered, S6 table, new S10)
for f in main_short supplement_short; do for i in 1 2 3; do pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode $f.tex; done; done
```

Result:
* 0 undefined references or citations and 0 overfull boxes in both logs.
* Both figures of the supplement are embedded; they are read from `../../figures/`, not copied.
* Underfull-box warnings come only from the bibliography, as in the full paper.

## Title and framing

* **Full title:** "The Loyola Index Family and the Resolution Limit of Degree Imbalance".
* **Short title:** "The Loyola Index Family and the Resolution Limit of Degree-Ratio Indices on Trees".
* **Short framing:** the research question is when the degree-ratio histogram stops determining the
  degree-pair counts of a tree. The Loyola family is the test case. The abstract says the class-wide
  dependence follows from Rada's structure relation, and that the minimal orders come from exhaustive
  enumeration.
